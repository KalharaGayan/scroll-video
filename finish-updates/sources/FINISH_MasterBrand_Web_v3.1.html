Why we exist

Nothing in his workup was abnormal. Everything about his trajectory was.

His cardiologist was pleased. His supplements were dialed in. His numbers were the best they had been in years. He had simply stopped walking three months earlier because his knee hurt, and he was waiting for it to settle down.

Ninety days of not moving costs strength, balance, weight and nerve. None of it appears on a panel. All of it decides whether he is still independent at eighty.

Healthspan is not won in the bloodwork. It is won or lost in whether you keep moving — and in who is available the week something starts to hurt.

The gap

Two industries, each holding half the answer.

The longevity clinic

Watches the decline in high resolution.

Excellent at measurement — panels, imaging, hormones, a beautifully rendered report. It cannot examine the joint that is currently deciding how far this man walks.

  • Thinks in decades

The orthopedic clinic

Closes the episode. The trajectory continues.

Excellent at the joint. It fixes the knee, discharges the patient, and never asks what the ninety days cost or whether he will still be walking in fifteen years.

  • Treats the injury
  • Acts this week
  • Not asking about the next fifty years

FINISH exists in the space between those two rooms — not as a compromise between them, but as the practice where the joint and the trajectory belong to the same conversation.

    Before you come in

    The questions people actually ask.

    Which pillar do I need?
    If something hurts, start with DirectOrtho+. If nothing hurts and you want to know where you stand, start with a FINISH Healthspan baseline. If you are not sure, call or text 706-224-9951. Sorting that out is our job, not yours.
    Do you take insurance?
    We do not bill insurance. FINISH is direct-pay: you pay a published price at the time of service. Services are HSA and FSA eligible, and an itemized superbill is available on request. Your insurance stays in place for surgery, advanced imaging and hospital care.
    Do I need a referral?
    No. No referral and no prior authorization. Call or text 706-224-9951 and describe what is going on.
    Do I need a membership to be seen?
    No. Every service is available on its own at a published price. Memberships exist for people who want ongoing care, and most of our patients will never hold one.
    Is FINISH Longevity open?
    Not yet. FINISH Longevity is in development and is not accepting patients, and no services or prices are offered for it. It opens only when four standards are met: named physician oversight, a written evidence position for every service, published pricing, and outcome tracking from day one.
