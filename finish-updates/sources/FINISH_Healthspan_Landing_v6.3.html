Health is not lived on a lab report. It is lived in motion.

Much of what shapes how you function in your seventies begins changing decades earlier, while there is still time to act on it. Waiting for a number to become abnormal is a decision, and usually the expensive one.

But a finding you cannot act on is just anxiety with a decimal point. The reason this pillar exists is that measuring and doing are the same job, and splitting them across a diagnostics company, a gym and a doctor’s office is why most people never close the loop.

Three ways this is usually sold, and what each one leaves out

Diagnostics without a path

Whole-body imaging. Over a hundred biomarkers. VO₂ max and resting metabolic rate. You can buy every one of these somewhere, and they are worth having — the problem is what happens next. You leave with a remarkable amount of information and no one whose job it is to help you act on any of it. The scan is the product; the follow-through is yours.

Medicine without performance

Concierge and longevity practices buy you a physician’s time and attention, which is worth having. What they generally do not include is anyone who programs your training, plans your food, or treats you when your shoulder stops working.

Coaching without medicine

A good coach changes your life. A good coach also cannot order labs, read a DEXA in clinical context, catch a thyroid problem behind your fatigue, or inject a joint. At some point the ceiling is medical.

We built the thing that sits in the middle: physician direction, year-round strength and nutrition coaching, physical therapy, and orthopedic access, priced as one fee rather than as an entry ticket with a menu of upcharges behind it.

Testing and assessment

VO₂ max$150

A graded test to exhaustion measuring how much oxygen your body can actually use. It is the reference measure of cardiorespiratory fitness, and it sets the heart-rate zones the rest of your training runs on.

Resting metabolic rate$100

Measures the calories you burn at rest by analysing your breath. Predictive equations are reasonable across a population and can be well off for any individual, so measuring yours removes a large source of error from a nutrition plan.

DEXA total body composition$175

A low-dose scan that separates your body into fat, lean mass and bone, region by region. A scale cannot tell you whether the weight you lost was fat or muscle. This can.

Lactate threshold$275

Blood samples across increasing workloads to find the intensity where fatigue begins to accelerate. It helps distinguish sustainable aerobic work from the intensities that accumulate fatigue much more quickly.

Functional movement and strength screen$200

A structured assessment of how you move and where you are restricted or weak. It gives your strength coach and physical therapist a shared starting point, and identifies what to address before you load it harder.

Movement follow-up (20 min)$75

A short re-check of the specific limitations found on your screen, to confirm the corrective work is doing what it was meant to do.

Screening

CT calcium score$300

A brief CT that quantifies calcified plaque in your coronary arteries. In adults without symptoms it predicts future cardiac events considerably better than a resting ECG. A score of zero carries a low ten-year event risk and is read alongside the rest of your risk picture rather than on its own.

At-home sleep apnea screen$400

A validated test you wear for one night in your own bed. Untreated apnea affects blood pressure, blood sugar, recovery and daytime function, and a large share of it goes undiagnosed in adults who consider themselves healthy.

Membership terms

Written plainly, because the point of publishing prices is lost if the terms are where the surprises live.

  • Twelve-month agreement with a six-month minimum, no exceptions. A member who cancels before month six remains liable through month six.
  • Pay monthly, quarterly, or in full. Paid in full saves 5%.
  • Individual services are FSA/HSA eligible. Membership dues are not.
  • Care credit, physical therapy visits and recovery sessions expire at the end of the membership year and do not roll over.
  • Membership is individual unless the tier states otherwise. Silver extends DirectOrtho+ to a spouse; Gold covers the household, including dependents under 26.
  • An existing DirectOrtho+ membership credits in full toward your first year here — $399 individual, $899 household. Value paid into one pillar applies toward the next as a rule, not as a negotiated exception.
  • No part of your dues is priced against FINISH Longevity and Wellness, which is not open. Membership establishes standing for entry when it opens, not access now.
