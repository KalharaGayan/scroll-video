The premise

Longevity is easy to turn into a catalog. We are building the opposite.

The point is not to collect more data. It is to know what deserves action.

A normal annual panel can coexist with a life that is quietly getting smaller. Strength changes. Bone changes. Sleep changes. Movement narrows. Recovery slows. Confidence shifts. Individually, none of those moments may look dramatic. Across ten years, they can become the story.

FINISH Longevity is being designed to hold that story in one place — on the same record, with the same team, and with the musculoskeletal foundation that makes every other longevity strategy usable.

The question is not simply how long you live. It is how much of yourself is still available when you get there.

The model

Measure. Decide. Act. Re-measure. Stay.

The longevity category often makes complexity look like sophistication. Our aim is the opposite: enough measurement to make a better decision, enough intervention to matter, and enough continuity to know whether it did.

01

Establish the trajectory

Begin with history, current capacity and the measurements that are clinically relevant — not every test simply because it exists.

02

Interpret the whole record

Orthopedic history, movement, bone, cardiometabolic data, recovery and performance belong in the same conversation rather than separate portals.

03

Act selectively

The future model will prioritize the smallest number of actions likely to matter. More interventions will not automatically mean better care.

04

Re-measure what should change

A second intervention should be decided from response and data, not enthusiasm. If something is not working, the plan should change.

05

Stay with the person

The value of longitudinal care is continuity: the clinician knows what changed, what did not, what was tried, and what mattered ten years ago.

Areas of longitudinal attention

These systems do not live in six boxes. Neither should your care.

Movement runs the whole length of a life. Bone has windows. Metabolic health changes direction. Sleep modulates everything above it. Cognition matters more as the years accumulate. And regenerative strategy enters occasionally and selectively, if at all — never as a permanent lane.

Area of attention EarlierMidlifeLater
Movement & capacityStrength, balance, mobility — the capacity to keep doing the things that make your life yours.
Bone & structural healthBone across the lifespan, with musculoskeletal function treated as infrastructure rather than an afterthought.
Metabolic & hormonal healthPatterns read as trajectories rather than isolated numbers, with clinical action only where it is warranted.
Sleep & recoveryWhether training, stress and everyday life are being absorbed — or quietly accumulating a cost.
Cognition & resilienceConsidered as part of whole-person function, alongside movement, metabolic health and daily capacity.
Regenerative strategyOnly where evidence and clinical context justify it — including an honest account of what the evidence does not show.

Illustrative, not a schedule, not a clinical timeline and not a scientific model. Where a lane begins is a matter of emphasis, not an age at which attention should start. These are areas of longitudinal attention, not a proprietary six-domain instrument and not a published service menu. Each future offering must clear an evidence, scope, pricing and outcome-tracking standard before it belongs here.

The value is not in the number of things watched. It is in the fact that the same people keep watching.

Category discipline

What FINISH Longevity will — and will not — become.

Built to become

A clinical relationship measured in years.

The model is intended to be deliberately small, longitudinal and accountable — with the patient, the record and the clinical rationale remaining intact as needs change.

  • One record across the FINISH ecosystem
  • Published rationale before intervention
  • Outcome tracking from the beginning
  • Transparent pricing when the model opens
  • Care that can say “you do not need this”

Will not become

A storefront for the newest longevity trend.

Novelty is not a clinical indication. The FINISH standard is meant to make it harder — not easier — for an exciting therapy to become a product before the evidence, scope and patient fit are clear.

  • A supplement sales channel
  • A subscription to tests nobody acts on
  • “Anti-aging” promises
  • A therapy menu disconnected from clinical need
  • Claims that outrun the evidence

Before it opens

The standard comes before the launch date.

We are not interested in being early if being early means being vague. FINISH Longevity will open only when the clinical model can meet the same transparency and accountability standards as the rest of FINISH.

Gate 01

Named physician oversight and documented scope

Every therapy must have a clear supervising structure and a defined clinical boundary.

Gate 02

An evidence position for every intervention

What it is for, who may benefit, what remains uncertain, and what it does not do.

Gate 03

Published pricing

Longevity does not get an exception from FINISH transparency because the category is premium.

Gate 04

Outcome tracking from day one

If we cannot define what success should look like and measure response, the intervention does not belong in the model.
