Our Story

A vision that began on a canyon trail.

The idea for FINISH didn't start in a boardroom. It started on a hike across the Grand Canyon.

What became clear on that trail was a practice built around keeping people moving — confidently and independently, for the whole of their lives, not just the length of a visit. Medicine measured by how well a person moves.

That vision became a clinic. The clinic became an ecosystem — precision musculoskeletal medicine, sports medicine and performance, and longevity, built as one continuous strategy for how a person moves from injury to recovery to lasting healthspan.

Our Team

Three co-founders. One shared conviction.

Ward · Farmer · Gaunder — Co-Founders

Three University of Georgia graduates and co-founders, home in Athens — bound by a shared conviction about how orthopedic and sports medicine care should be delivered, and by decades of history that runs deeper than a business. Each leads the discipline he knows best, which is what makes this a team rather than a practice with one name on the door.

Kelly Ward, MMSc, PA-C

Co-Founder & Clinical Director

Precision Musculoskeletal Medicine · Athens

Kelly Ward is a co-founder and the full-time clinical leader of FINISH Performance Health, with 28 years in orthopedics and sports medicine in Athens. The same clinician performs your exam, reads your imaging, builds your plan, and follows your case. A University of Georgia graduate, he completed his physician assistant training at Emory University, began practicing as an orthopedic PA in 1998, and served alongside UGA Athletics for 26 years — working with athletes at every level from youth to elite competition.

One of those athletes was Chris Gaunder. Kelly was his orthopedic PA-C during his playing days at Georgia, followed his career through medical school, residency, and a decade of military service, and ultimately recruited him home to Athens as a co-founder of this practice. It is the kind of relationship FINISH is built on.

His conviction is simple and hard-won: most musculoskeletal problems never need surgery — they need time, an accurate diagnosis, and the right conservative-first plan. FINISH is the practice he built to deliver exactly that.

Matthew Farmer, MD

Co-Founder & Medical Director

Board-Certified Internal Medicine

Dr. Matthew Farmer serves as Medical Director of FINISH Performance Health, bringing 14 years of experience in internal medicine to the practice. A University of Georgia graduate, he earned his medical degree at the Medical College of Georgia.

He provides medical oversight across the FINISH ecosystem. His presence is what lets a musculoskeletal evaluation here sit inside a complete picture of your health — not apart from it — and it anchors the whole-person, conservative-first philosophy at the center of everything FINISH does.

As Medical Director, Dr. Farmer provides the clinical governance that holds FINISH to a standard beyond the minimum: the same standard the team would demand for their own care.

Chris Gaunder, MD

Co-Founder & Director, FINISH Longevity

Board-Certified Orthopedic Surgery

A co-founder of FINISH Performance Health, Dr. Chris Gaunder brings orthopedic surgical perspective to the founding table — which means the conservative-first standard here was set with a surgeon in the room. He is board certified in orthopedic surgery and a proud University of Georgia alumnus — a Biology major who lettered in football on the 2005 SEC Championship team before earning his medical degree at Mercer University and completing orthopedic surgery residency at Brooke Army Medical Center. He served ten years as a Major in the United States Air Force.

His path to FINISH runs back to those same playing days at Georgia, and to the clinician who treated him there. Decades later, that relationship brought him home to Athens as a co-founder of this organization, where he also directs the FINISH Longevity pillar.

Dr. Gaunder's surgical practice is separate and independent of FINISH Performance Health. He does not see or treat patients through DirectOrtho+, and provides no orthopedic clinical care, surgical services or patient consultation here. His contribution is at the founding and strategic level, including direction of the FINISH Longevity pillar.

How We're Built

Independent by design.

A practice accountable to the patient in the room.

FINISH DirectOrtho+ is an independent, direct-pay non-surgical orthopedic and sports medicine practice. No surgical services are performed by the practice. Care is delivered by a physician-supervised clinical team led by Kelly Ward, MMSc, PA-C, consistent with Georgia requirements, with Dr. Matthew Farmer serving as Medical Director. Dr. Christopher Gaunder's role is limited to direction of the FINISH Longevity pillar and strategic advisory; he does not participate in orthopedic patient care at DirectOrtho+.

This structure is deliberate. We own no MRI scanner, no CT scanner, and no surgery center, so nothing downstream of your visit changes what we earn. We do not bill insurance, so no coverage determination sits between a clinician and a plan. When cost and incentive are settled before you arrive, the only question left in the room is what your body actually needs — and that is what we get to spend the visit on.

It is the difference between a visit that ends in a disposition — where you go next — and one that ends in a decision: what is wrong, what it needs, and what happens now. That is medicine practiced the way it was meant to be: unhurried, transparent, and centered on the patient in the room.
